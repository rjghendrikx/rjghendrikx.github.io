A canal house at night, seen from the front room's table: two tall sash windows with transom lights look out over a dark, rain-ruffled canal that mirrors the street lamps and the lit windows of the houses across the water, with moored boats along the quays; inside, a small projector on a stand aims at a white plaster head of Plato on a plinth to the right, a workbench under the windows, in warm lamplight.
