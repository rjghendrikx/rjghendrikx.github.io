How it works

  1. Find someone talkingPut on what you normally watch and wait for one person talking, face to you. The news works well.
  2. Film the TVPoint your phone at the face and tap Film. Stay quiet. Synclipse stops by itself when it is sure, in under a minute.
  3. Read the resultIn sync, slightly off or out of sync, whether the sound is early or late, and how to change the audio delay. Change it, check again, and see if you got closer.

Any TV, any soundbar, any show

Synclipse watches the lips and listens to the speech, so it needs no cables and nothing special on the TV. It needs a real person talking: cartoons, dubbed films and music don't work.

When a recording isn't good enough, it says so and tells you why, instead of guessing.

Where do I change it?

Samsung, LG, Sony, Sonos, Bose, Denon, Apple TV and more: the audio delay guide shows per brand where the setting is, and what to do when the sound is late and the delay is already at 0.

New to this? Read why the sound is out of sync with the picture and how to make your TV's picture faster.

Want it precise?

Play the included test video on your TV the way you watch films and Fine-tune measures the delay within a few milliseconds, for that way of playing.

On your phone

The measuring happens on your phone. Synclipse has no account, no ads and no tracking, and sends nothing to anyone. The details are in the privacy policy.
