Sound before the picture on your TV? Make the picture faster

By Ruud Hendrikx, maker of Synclipse. Updated 28 November 2026.

When the sound is early (ahead of the lips), the picture takes too long. Switch off the TV's picture options, motion smoothing first, and the picture arrives sooner. No maker says by how much, so check after each change.

Only for sound that is early. With sound that is late, this makes it worse: read why the sound is late instead. Not sure which it is? Synclipse tells you which way and by about how many milliseconds (ms): film someone talking on your TV with your iPhone.

Would you rather keep the picture options on? Raise the audio delay instead: where it is, per brand.

What do I switch off to make the picture faster?

Motion smoothing, noise reduction and sharpening. The picture mode Filmmaker Mode switches off those three together; or switch the picture options off one by one, motion smoothing first.

One by one, in this order:

  1. Motion smoothing. Every brand has its own name for it: Auto Motion Plus (Samsung), TruMotion (LG), Motionflow (Sony), Motion styles (Philips).
  2. Noise reduction.
  3. Sharpness, edge enhancement and other extras that make the picture sharper.
  4. Dynamic contrast and colour enhancement.

Change one thing, then check again with Synclipse. No better? Set it back.

No Filmmaker Mode on your TV? Try the picture mode Movie or Cinema. On a Samsung TV, Filmmaker Mode may look darker than other picture modes.

Sony TV? Look at A/V sync first: Sony says this setting delays the picture. The path is under Sony below.

Game mode comes last, and only if nothing else helped. See game mode, below.

Where are these settings on my TV?

Menu names are as the maker writes them. They can differ per model and software version. More options per brand are in the guide under make the picture faster.

Samsung

Settings › All Settings › Picture › Picture Mode › FILMMAKER MODE

The picture may look darker than in other picture modes.

Settings › Picture › Expert Settings › Auto Motion Plus Settings (Picture Clarity Settings)

Motion smoothing. It starts on Auto: choose Off.

Settings › Picture or System (depends on the model) › Game Mode

Game mode. Samsung says it also sets the sound mode to Game, and that it is not suitable for regular TV viewing.

LG

FILMMAKER MODE

LG says it turns off motion smoothing and sharpening.

All Settings › Picture › Advanced Settings › Clarity › TruMotion

2021 to 2024 models (webOS 6.0 to webOS 24). LG's motion smoothing: choose Off. LG says, about games, that changing motion settings can increase input lag. In the same menu: Super Resolution, Noise Reduction, MPEG Noise Reduction and Smooth Gradation.

Settings › General › Game Optimizer

Game mode on many models from 2021 (webOS 6.0 and later). LG says it may lock or limit some picture options while it is on.

Sony

Settings › Display & Sound › Audio output › A/V sync

Sony says this setting holds the picture back. Sound early? Try Off.

Settings › Display & Sound › Picture › Motion › Motionflow › Off

Sony is not on the list of brands with Filmmaker Mode. Switch off the single options. Google TV models. Motionflow is Sony's motion smoothing. On some models Motion sits under Picture › Advanced.

Settings › Display & Sound › Picture › Picture mode › Game

Game mode on Google TV models. Sony says A/V sync on Auto does not work in this mode. On the newest BRAVIA models it is then fixed to Off.

Philips

Settings › Display and sound › Picture › Picture style or AI Picture style › Filmmaker Mode

Google TV models, 2025. On 2023 models the style for films is called Movie.

Settings › Display and sound › Picture › Motion › Motion styles

Google TV models, 2025. Motion smoothing: choose Off. In the picture styles Game and Monitor this is Game motion styles, where Philips names the option Off (lowest latency).

Home › Settings › Picture › Expert mode settings

2023 models (PML9008). Here: Motion Styles (choose Off), Noise reduction, MPEG Artefact Reduction, Ultra Resolution and Dynamic contrast.

Home › Settings › Picture › Viewing mode

2026 models (OLED851): Game or Monitor. Philips says Monitor shows the picture with minimum processing. On other models Game and Monitor are picture styles.

TCL

Settings › Picture › Advanced settings

Google TV models. Look for Motion or Motion Clarity and turn Motion Smoothing or Action Smoothing off. TCL says motion smoothing can delay the picture.

Settings › Picture › Game Mode or Low Latency Mode

Game mode on Google TV models. TCL says it can also change how motion looks. Each HDMI input has its own setting.

Hisense

Settings › Picture › Picture Mode Settings

Older VIDAA models (US manual, A6GV). Here: Ultra Smooth Motion, Clear Motion, Noise Reduction, MPEG Noise Reduction and Adaptive Contrast. Hisense says in a guide about gaming that switching off options that are heavy on the processor reduces input lag.

Settings › Picture › Picture Mode › Game

Game mode on older VIDAA models (US manual, A6GV). Hisense says Game Mode can also change the colours and lower the picture quality.

Panasonic

Home › Settings › Display and Sounds › Picture Settings › HDMI Settings › Input Lag

Choose Fast. It sits under HDMI Settings, and Panasonic says it does not work in TV mode. In the picture modes Game and True Game it is already on Fast. From Panasonic's US help page, which names no models.

Not listed? Look in the TV's picture settings for the options above.

Should I use game mode for films and TV?

Try it last. Game mode makes the picture faster too, but it changes more: how the picture looks, and on some TVs also the sound or how the TV matches picture and sound. The difference between picture and sound can even grow. Samsung says its Game Mode is not suitable for regular TV viewing.

Which picture options delay the picture?

No maker says how much each option gains, and none gives a number in ms. TCL says motion smoothing can add delay. LG says, about games, that changing motion settings can increase input lag.

Hisense says in a guide about gaming that switching off options that are heavy on the processor reduces input lag. That is why you check after each change, and set back what did not help.

The sound is still early. What else can I do?

Raise the audio delay on the device that plays the sound. Most TVs and soundbars can delay sound, and that fixes sound that is early.
