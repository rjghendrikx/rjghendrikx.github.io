Synclipse Privacy Policy

Last updated 6 November 2026

Synclipse is an iPhone app that measures whether the sound of your TV is in sync with the picture. It is made by Ruud Hendrikx in the Netherlands. This page says what the app does with your data. The short version: Synclipse collects nothing. Everything it records and measures stays on your iPhone.

What Synclipse collects

Nothing. Synclipse has no account, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking and no crash reporting service of its own. The app makes no internet connections, and it sends nothing to the developer or to anyone else.

Camera and microphone

Synclipse asks for the camera and microphone so it can film your TV and hear its sound. When you tap Film, it records a short video: 15 seconds, or 7 seconds when you measure with the test video. While you aim, the iPhone's built-in face detection shows where a face is in the picture.

The analysis runs on your iPhone. Synclipse finds the face and measures how far the mouth opens in each frame, and compares that with the speech it hears. It does not recognise who someone is. With the test video it looks for its flashes and clicks instead.

Videos you choose yourself

You can also measure a video you filmed earlier. You pick it with Apple's photo picker or the Files app. Synclipse then copies only that video into its own folder, so it can measure it. It cannot see the rest of your photos.

To measure with the test video, you can save it to your photo library, so you can play it on your TV. For that, Synclipse asks only to add to your photos, not to read them.

What is stored, and where

Synclipse keeps these files in its own Documents folder on your iPhone:

  • the videos it records, and copies of videos you chose to measure;
  • the result of each measurement, so you can look at earlier results again.

It also keeps your settings on the iPhone: for example the style and how strict a result should be.

The Documents folder is visible to you in the Files app, under On My iPhone > Synclipse. Like other app data, it is included in your iCloud or computer backup if you have backups turned on. Those backups are between you and Apple; the developer has no access to them.

How long, and how to delete it

Recordings and results stay until you delete them. Synclipse removes a recording itself only when you cancel it or when it fails. To delete recordings or results, open the Files app, go to On My iPhone > Synclipse, and delete the files you no longer want. To delete everything, including your settings, delete the Synclipse app.

Sharing

Synclipse shares nothing on its own. If you share or save a file yourself, for example the test video through the share sheet, you choose where it goes. That copy is then handled by the app or service you chose.

Third parties

Synclipse contains no third-party code, and it gives your data to no one. If you have turned on sharing analytics with app developers in the iPhone's settings, Apple may pass anonymous crash reports to the developer. You control that in Settings > Privacy & Security > Analytics & Improvements.

Children

Synclipse collects no data from anyone, children included.

Changes

If what the app does with data changes, this page will change with it, and the date at the top will show when.
