deck-a.mp3, deck-b.mp3 - the decks' two records (layer-deck.js), no recordings
  Synthesised offline from the records' own score (tools/render_records.py): oscillators, filtered noise, and a
  reverb whose impulse response is generated there too (decaying noise and early reflections). No samples, no
  third-party audio. 0.5 s of pre-roll with one tone burst (the page's alignment mark), never played.
